### Would do differently

1. **One architect pass** for “follow-up answers wrong/absent”: retrieve query, generate synonymy, and draft-level `query_log` (plus OpenRouter `gen-…` ids) specified together — not a chain of retrieve-only addenda.
2. **Do not ship an answer prompt from a notebook** that omits `__facts__` / live conversation until a live turn with logged **draft** confirms it. Keep the nearby-topic abstain for true nearby facts; name synonymy in the spec so it is not treated as leakage.
